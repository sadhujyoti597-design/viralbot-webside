ViralBot Privacy Policy

```

Last updated: October 2026

ViralBot is an AI-assisted content generation and publishing tool. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use ViralBot and its integrations with third-party services such as Meta.

Information We Access

When you connect ViralBot to a Facebook Page, ViralBot may access information necessary to manage and publish content to that Page. This may include Page identifiers, Page names, access permissions, and information made available through the Meta Graph API.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through Meta is used only to provide the features of ViralBot, including generating content and publishing authorized content to connected Facebook Pages.

Access Tokens

Access tokens are used to authenticate authorized requests to third-party services. Users should keep their access tokens private. ViralBot does not intentionally publish access tokens or other authentication credentials.

Data Storage

ViralBot may store information required to operate the service. Information is not intentionally sold or rented to third parties.

Third-Party Services

ViralBot may use third-party services, including Meta's services, to provide social media publishing functionality. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of ViralBot. Instructions are available on our User Data Deletion page.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by ViralBot. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ViralBot's functionality changes or when necessary to reflect changes in applicable requirements.

Contact

For privacy or data deletion requests, please use the contact information provided by the ViralBot developer.
```
