Last updated: October 2026

Request Data Deletion

If you have used ViralBot and want your information associated with the service deleted, you can request deletion of your data.

How to Request Deletion

Send a request to the ViralBot developer using the contact method provided by the developer. Include enough information to identify your account or connection, but do not send passwords or access tokens.

What Happens After Your Request

We will review the request and delete information that is eligible for deletion, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, security, or legitimate operational purposes.

Facebook / Meta Connections

You can also remove ViralBot's access to your Facebook account or Pages through the relevant Facebook settings. Removing access prevents ViralBot from making future authorized requests using that connection.

Contact

For data deletion requests or privacy questions, contact the ViralBot developer through the contact information associated with the application.
